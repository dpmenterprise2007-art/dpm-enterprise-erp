# DPM ERP Development Roadmap

## Sprint 0 – Foundation
Repository, application skeleton, database service, architecture and security baseline.

## Sprint 1 – Core masters
Company profile, financial year, authentication/RBAC, customers, vendors, items, units, HSN/SAC and tax masters.

## Sprint 2 – Sales documents
Quotations, estimates, draft GST invoices, validation, numbering, PDF output, credit/debit notes.

## Sprint 3 – Accounting engine
Chart of accounts, journal headers/lines, atomic balanced posting, receipts, payments, contra and journal vouchers, ledgers.

## Sprint 4 – Financial reports
Trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, cash/bank books, receivables/payables ageing, bank reconciliation.

## Sprint 5 – Procurement, GST and inventory
Purchase bills, vendor settlement, ITC tracking, GST reconciliation exports, stock ledger and valuation.

## Sprint 6 – DPM workflows
Project register, BOQ, budgets, material/labour/site cost allocation, furniture BOM and production orders.

## Sprint 7 – Desktop and operations
Tauri Windows packaging, local deployment, encrypted off-site backup, restore drills and release checklist.

## Production release gates
Tests for tax arithmetic, numbering concurrency, balanced journals, payment allocation, ledger reconciliation, access control, backup restore and desktop packaging.
